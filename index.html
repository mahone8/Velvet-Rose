Who We Are

A residence built
for women, by design

Tucked into the quiet grid of Sector F-8, The Velvet Rose Girls Hostel was created for students and professional women who want more than four walls — a residence that is genuinely safe, thoughtfully furnished, and quietly luxurious. Every room is curated, every corridor is watched over, and every detail is chosen to make you feel looked after.

From your first enquiry to move-in day, our team handles the details so you can focus on your studies, your career, and your city.

Our Mission

To give every resident a secure, comfortable, and dignified home in Islamabad — backed by round-the-clock care and honest service.

Our Vision

To be Islamabad's most trusted name in women's residential living — where luxury, safety, and community live under one roof.
